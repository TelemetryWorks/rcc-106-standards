# RCC 124-17 archival decision — 2026-11-06

The owner authorized adding the recovered January 2017 handbook. The reviewed
choice is **one edition/release, `rcc-124-17`, with two distinct source variants**.
The [dated investigation](2026-11-05-rcc124-17.md) and its original evidence
remain unchanged apart from a follow-up link. This archival decision does not

Canonical denotes the archive's default filename, not greater authenticity.
The vendor PDF reports a 2017-01-31 modification date; the Archive PDF reports
2018-01-16 and an iText-modified producer. Neither date establishes how much
was rewritten, which copy is more final, or an official chain of custody.
Keeping both containers preserves these different provenances without
inventing a second edition or overwriting another document.

Both manifest records retain the **January 2017** visible edition, 163 pages,
the reported creation date 2017-01-31, and the exact visible cover marking:
**DISTRIBUTION A: APPROVED FOR PUBLIC RELEASE, DISTRIBUTION IS UNLIMITED**.
Both also retain the caveat that their PDF metadata Title says **(Draft)
TMATS User's Guide (Draft)**. No metadata, watermark or PDF bytes were edited.

The Archive item is a community-uploaded copy labeled DTIC AD1025657, not a
direct DTIC download. Its advertised size/SHA-1/MD5 matched the received file;
these are the mirror's identifiers, not official byte authentication. The
manifest retains its stable download URL and the actually observed matching
[redirect endpoint](https://dn760104.eu.archive.org/0/items/DTIC_AD1025657/DTIC_AD1025657.pdf).
That host may move; the stable URL remains the first original-source fallback.
Each record uses only URLs observed to return **its own** bytes. The two PDFs
must not be substituted for one another despite their matching text.

Original WSMR and inaccessible DTIC references remain in the investigation
and notes, not as falsely verified byte-identical download sources. Official
RCC citations and Science.gov corroborate identity only; their full URLs and
the direct-source failures are retained in the dated investigation.
